1.3. Thể nghi vấn
                     Have        I/ you/ we/ they             V (ed/ cột 3)?
                     Has         she/ he/ it                  V (ed/ cột 3)?
Cách trả lời
                                       + Yes, I/ you/ we/ they have.
                                         Yes, she/ he/ it has.
                                       + No, I/ you/ we/ they haven’t.
                                         No, she/ he/ it hasn’t.
